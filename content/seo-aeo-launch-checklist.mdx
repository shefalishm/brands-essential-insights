## Discovery and indexing

- Production pages return the intended status codes.
- Robots rules do not block required pages or assets.
- Canonical URLs use the final HTTPS domain consistently.
- The XML sitemap contains indexable canonical URLs only.
- Navigation and contextual links make important pages discoverable.

## Page meaning

- Every important page serves a distinct search and customer intent.
- Titles, descriptions, main headings and URLs are descriptive and unique.
- Copy answers the primary question clearly before expanding into detail.
- Claims have first-hand or cited evidence and meaningful update dates.
- Images have accurate alternative text where needed.

## Structured information

- Organisation details are consistent across the site.
- Article, breadcrumb or service markup matches visible content.
- Structured data validates without inventing ratings, reviews or FAQs.
- Social share images and metadata render correctly.

## Conversion and measurement

- Each page offers a logical next action.
- Forms are accessible, spam-protected and routed to the right team.
- Analytics records agreed conversions without capturing sensitive content.
- Consent and privacy handling match the markets served.
- Baselines and post-launch review dates are documented.

## After launch

Inspect priority URLs, submit the sitemap, monitor crawl and indexing patterns, and test real enquiries. A launch checklist reduces avoidable errors; ongoing relevance, evidence and iteration create durable visibility.
