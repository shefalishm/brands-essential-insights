## The bottleneck is usually extraction

Founders often have rich context and very little time. Asking them to write from a blank page creates a system that fails under pressure.

## Design around conversation

Start with a short recorded discussion around one customer problem. Use a clear prompt, ask for examples, and capture the founder’s language before polishing it.

From that source, create:

- One substantive article or newsletter
- Two or three focused social posts
- A sales-enablement note for the team
- A bank of proof points and examples

## Protect the voice

AI can organise, compress, and create variants. It should not erase the founder’s opinions, specificity, or natural rhythm. A small voice guide makes review faster and keeps the content recognisable.
