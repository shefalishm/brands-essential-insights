## Optimise for understanding

Search and answer systems both benefit when your expertise is explicit. Clear definitions, focused pages, direct answers, and credible examples help people and machines understand what your business knows.

## Publish what only you can know

Generic summaries are abundant. Original value comes from real process, informed opinion, useful data, and specific outcomes.

### Strengthen the evidence layer

Name the author. Date meaningful updates. Explain how conclusions were reached. Connect claims to cases, methods, or sources. Use structured data to reinforce what the page already says.

AI visibility is not a separate trick. It is a new reason to practice disciplined communication.
