## Message

- The opening states who the business helps and the outcome offered.
- Language reflects customer problems rather than internal terminology.
- Important services are distinguishable without reading every paragraph.
- The primary action is specific and appropriate to the buying stage.

## Trust

- Claims are supported by relevant work, credentials, testimonials or process.
- Proof appears near the decision it supports.
- Contact details, policies and company identity are easy to verify.
- Case studies distinguish verified results from general examples.

## Journey

- Navigation uses labels a customer will understand.
- Headings tell a coherent story when scanned alone.
- Links guide visitors to focused service and evidence pages.
- The contact path explains what happens after enquiry.

## Search and answer readiness

- The title and description accurately represent the page.
- One logical main heading introduces the business.
- Important information is present in crawlable text.
- Organisation details are consistent and structured data reflects visible facts.

## Experience and measurement

- The page is usable by keyboard and readable on small screens.
- Images are purposeful, appropriately compressed and described when meaningful.
- The page loads quickly under realistic mobile conditions.
- Forms, phone links, email links and analytics events work.

Prioritise any failure that blocks understanding or action. Record the baseline, make the change, and evaluate qualified enquiries—not clicks in isolation.
