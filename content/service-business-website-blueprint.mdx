## The representative challenge

Imagine a specialist firm whose homepage lists capabilities but never explains which customer situations it solves. Most enquiries arrive through referrals, organic traffic lands on one broad services page, and prospects repeatedly ask basic fit questions.

## The diagnostic

Interview leadership and sales, review search demand, map current pages and analyse enquiry quality. The likely issue is not simply visual age: the site lacks a decision structure connecting audience problems, services, proof and action.

## The proposed transformation

1. Write a clear positioning statement grounded in real customer language.
2. Create one focused page for each priority service and buying intent.
3. Place relevant case evidence beside important claims.
4. Publish practical answers to recurring evaluation questions.
5. Replace a generic contact form with a short, expectation-setting enquiry path.
6. Add measurement for qualified form submissions and booked conversations.

## What a real case should measure

Record organic visibility for service topics, engagement with proof, form completion, lead qualification and progression to a sales conversation. Compare like periods and note other changes that may influence results.

This blueprint shows the reasoning a strong engagement might follow. It intentionally makes no performance claim. Credibility is more valuable than a dramatic but unsupported number.
