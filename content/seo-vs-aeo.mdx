## SEO earns discovery; AEO earns inclusion in an answer

SEO improves the chance that a page appears for a relevant search. AEO improves the chance that its information can be understood, trusted and used in a direct or AI-generated response. Buyers move between both behaviours, so treating them as rival strategies creates unnecessary duplication.

## The shared foundation

Both disciplines depend on a technically accessible website, pages mapped to genuine intent, descriptive headings, useful internal links and credible information. Thin pages created only to repeat keywords help neither.

The strongest content often includes:

- A concise answer near the question
- A deeper explanation for evaluation
- Original examples, methods or evidence
- A clear author or organisational source
- Relevant dates and meaningful updates
- A next step for readers who need help

## Where the work differs

Classic SEO includes keyword demand, search-result presentation, links and organic landing-page performance. AEO places extra attention on question phrasing, extractable explanations, entity clarity and evidence that another system can verify.

Structured data can describe content already visible on the page. It is supporting information, not a substitute for substance and not a guarantee of a special search result or AI citation.

## A sensible order of investment

Fix crawling, indexing, site structure and service-page clarity first. Then build topic clusters around commercial and educational questions. Strengthen those pages with expertise, comparison points, FAQs and original proof. Finally, monitor how the brand appears across search and answer experiences.

The commercial goal is not to be mentioned everywhere. It is to be discovered and trusted at the moments that influence a qualified decision.
