## The challenge

The team was active across several channels but lacked a shared message, a clear publishing rhythm, and an easy way to decide what deserved attention.

## The approach

We simplified the offer story, grouped content around three customer questions, and designed one monthly workflow from expert input to distribution.

### What changed

- One message guide replaced inconsistent channel briefs.
- Four strong monthly ideas replaced a high-volume calendar.
- Every asset had a clear role in the customer journey.
- A lightweight review step kept quality and accountability with the team.

## The result

Marketing became easier to plan and easier to evaluate. The team spent less time starting from scratch and more time improving ideas that mattered.
