## The brief

Copy these prompts into your working document and keep each answer short.

1. **Business objective:** What needs to change?
2. **Audience:** Whose behaviour are we trying to influence?
3. **Insight:** What is true about their current situation?
4. **Single-minded message:** What is the one idea they should remember?
5. **Proof:** Why should they believe it?
6. **Action:** What exactly should they do next?
7. **Channels:** Where can the idea meet them with the right context?
8. **Measurement:** Which signal would show meaningful progress?

## The discipline

If the brief cannot fit on one page, the decision-making probably is not finished. Add detail in supporting documents; keep the strategic core easy for everyone to see.
