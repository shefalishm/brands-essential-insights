## Choose with one automation you can test

Shortlist ActiveCampaign when a business needs to coordinate several actions and handoffs after a signup. Shortlist Mailchimp when newsletter production and a defined set of automations describe the core work. Compare both on the actual plan and audience you would operate.

The official pricing pages were checked November 9, 2026. ActiveCampaign lists different automation features and limits by tier. Mailchimp's marketing plans also vary by automation and sending capabilities. Ask each vendor to show the required sequence on the tier being quoted. [ActiveCampaign plans](https://www.activecampaign.com/pricing) and [Mailchimp marketing plans](https://mailchimp.com/pricing/marketing/)

## Draw the journey before opening the builder

Imagine an independent workshop that offers a maintenance guide and separately accepts service enquiries. A visitor requests the guide and agrees to the stated email programme. The business sends the resource, follows up with one useful tip and provides a clear route to ask about service.

Write the entry event, data collected, actions and exit conditions in ordinary language. Decide what happens if the person submits the same form again or makes an enquiry before the follow-up. Agree whether the next message should still be sent and who owns the answer. Use those decisions as the brief for both demonstrations.

### Test normal and exceptional paths

Create a permitted test record and complete the website signup. Check what the visitor sees, where the record appears, what permission information is retained and whether the expected message arrives. Check the link and reply address. Verify what happens when someone unsubscribes before a later scheduled message.

Repeat the test with a duplicate email address and an existing contact. Check whether the automation re-enters and whether the business can explain the result. Include an integration failure in the review. Ask how staff can see the error, retry safely and confirm the final state.

## Compare the entire cost at the same scale

Make a sheet with audience counts, expected monthly sends, required workflow steps, users, integrations and optional channels. Ask each vendor which records count, when limits are assessed and how it handles overages or tier changes. Keep marketing permission and billable contact status as separate fields. [Mailchimp contact types](https://mailchimp.com/help/about-your-contacts/)

Use a current quote for your country and billing period. Include implementation, template setup, website form changes, sender-domain work and the staff time needed to review campaigns. Add a second scenario for a busier month using assumptions you can explain. Compare the full cost and identify which requirements trigger a more expensive tier.

## Plan a move around permission and continuity

Inventory forms, permission records, tags, excluded addresses, active automations and the people responsible. Decide what must move, what should remain in an approved archive and which fields have different meanings between systems. Verify how the proposed tool can retain required suppressions before allowing live sending.

Run a small migration with authorised test records. Compare the results against the original records and follow a signup through the new journey. Plan when the old form destination will stop receiving new signups and when the replacement will start. Assign a person to review records received around the switch and decide how to recover if the new connection fails. ## Put the ongoing work in the decision

Ask the intended editor to revise a message and verify its audience, links and next action. Ask the backup operator to find an automation, explain why a record entered it and show how to pause it. Record which tasks require a specialist and what the business will pay for that work.

## Turn the sample journey into a test sheet

Record a case for a new signup, a duplicate signup, an enquiry after the first email and an unsubscribe before the next email. For each, specify the starting contact state, the website action, the message expected and the final state. For example, an existing subscriber who requests the guide a second time may need the guide again while staying out of the full welcome series. Agree on that behaviour before evaluating how a tool implements it.

Confirm which events and properties the website can supply. A form can capture an address and an explicit selection, but a different system may own a later service enquiry. Ask the builder to show how that event reaches the automation and what happens if it arrives late. If the two tools identify the same person differently, write down the matching rule. Ask how staff can review ambiguous cases without accidentally changing marketing permission.

### Compare plans at two plausible audience sizes

Give vendors an ordinary month and a busier month using the same assumptions. Include expected active contacts, other stored records, total sends, staff access and required automation actions. Separate newsletters from welcome and follow-up sends so the bidder can explain how the figures were counted. If you require a sales pipeline or another channel, ask which tier or add-on provides it and include that charge explicitly.

Record the price and billing term at the time of evaluation. Ask what happens when contact or sending limits are exceeded and how quickly a team can pause or revise an unexpectedly busy workflow. Give the backup operator a clear way to locate the current send plan and check whether an upcoming message still makes sense.

### Give cutover and recovery separate owners

Assign one person to the live website form, one to the email platform and one to approving the migration. The same person can own several tasks if the responsibilities are clear. Test a representative record before switching the form. Save enough evidence to tell whether new records after cutover reached the correct audience and whether follow-up was sent once.

Keep an agreed recovery step for a form that stops delivering records. Identify where incoming submissions are stored, who can correct the destination and how the team will compare the records collected during the interruption. Pause affected automations while discrepancies are reviewed. Test the restored workflow before enabling normal sending again. Make those steps part of the handover so the business can operate the system after the initial setup.

Start with one reliable signup and follow-up sequence. Add branches after the business can name the customer behaviour that justifies them.
