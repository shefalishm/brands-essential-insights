## Begin with the work, not the tools

The best AI stack is not the one with the most subscriptions. It is the one that reduces friction in a workflow your team understands.

Map the recurring work first: research, planning, drafting, production, distribution, and measurement. Then identify where speed matters and where judgment matters more.

## A three-layer model

### 1. Thinking support

Use AI to surface patterns, structure messy notes, compare angles, and pressure-test a brief. Keep a person responsible for the conclusion.

### 2. Production support

Create repeatable starting points for outlines, variations, repurposing, and quality checks. Your brand system should guide every output.

### 3. Operational support

Automate hand-offs, naming, summaries, and reporting where the rules are stable. Avoid automating important customer decisions without review.

## The final check

For every tool, ask: does this make the work clearer, faster, or more effective? If the answer is merely “it can generate things,” it probably does not belong in the stack yet.
