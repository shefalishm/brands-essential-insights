## The representative challenge

A multi-location service company wants more local enquiries. Its existing pages repeat the same copy with a different place name, offer little evidence of local work and compete with each other for similar searches.

## The proposed system

Start by validating where services, teams and customer needs actually differ. Define a reusable page structure while requiring meaningful local inputs:

- Services available in that area
- Specific response or delivery constraints
- Locally relevant projects or testimonials with approval
- Team, office or service-area information
- Common questions unique to the location
- Consistent name, address and contact details where applicable

Use regional hubs when several nearby areas share the same information. Link between service and location pages only where the relationship helps a visitor.

## Quality controls

Assign one canonical destination per intent, prevent empty pages from being indexed and include pages in the sitemap only when they are ready. Keep business information consistent across the site and authoritative profiles.

## How a real case would prove value

Measure qualified enquiries by location, organic landing-page performance, direction or phone actions where relevant, and lead quality. Separate brand demand from non-brand discovery and document any offline campaign influence.

This is a planning example, not a claim about a client. Its purpose is to show how local SEO can scale without sacrificing accuracy or usefulness.
