# September 22, 2026: city website guides

## Scope and methodology

Five new standalone articles, each over 2,500 body words, six FAQs, unique metadata, internal links and a contact CTA. These are distinct business-workflow guides, not fabricated client case studies. No local offices, sales results, testimonials or package prices are invented. No em dashes. Existing templates supply Article, FAQ and breadcrumb schema, canonical URLs and sitemap entries.

Cities are a qualitative smaller-market editorial selection. Search results showed existing competitors in all five. No keyword-volume or paid difficulty dataset was available, so lower competition is not verified. Rankings, indexing and AI citations are not guaranteed.
